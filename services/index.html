Digital Twin Training

UniversitiesMRO OfficialsIndividuals

Live, instructor-led training on building digital twins — taught by the people who actually built PHI-Twin, not a licensed curriculum reseller. Three pricing tiers depending on who you are:

  • University departments — full-cohort pricing, custom curriculum scope
  • MRO officials — professional-track pricing, applied case studies from real aircraft components
  • Individuals — self-enrollment pricing, same material as the professional track

Sessions run over our own screen-share app (see School Apps below). In-person delivery is available if you cover travel costs. On enrollment we send a meeting link and course access directly — no separate app download required to start.

Lifestones — Autonomous Sermon Display

Tauri v2 / RustOffline Speech RecognitionWindows · macOS · Linux

We built this because existing church presentation software makes you route through OBS and manually cue every verse. Lifestones listens to the sermon locally (no cloud, no internet dependency), recognizes when a Bible verse is being referenced, and displays it automatically — no operator required, no heavyweight video-routing setup.
