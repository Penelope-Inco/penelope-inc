PHI-06 — AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS & UAV VALIDATION

PHI-Drone: a flight-test range that exists entirely in software, before a single motor spins.

Hardware-in-the-loop and software-in-the-loop validation for autonomous UAV trajectory planning, vision-based object recognition, and failure-mode recovery — built to find what breaks before a real airframe does.

Flight-testing autonomy is expensive and slow when every trajectory-planning bug or vision-recognition edge case costs you a real airframe, a real flight, and real risk. PHI-Drone treats simulation as the proving ground, not an afterthought.

The framework integrates ArduPilot's software-in-the-loop simulator with FlightGear for realistic flight dynamics and YOLOv8 for real-time object detection and tracking, so trajectory planning, adaptive flight control, and failure-mitigation logic get stress-tested thousands of times before anything touches real hardware.

Every simulated flight logs full MAVLink telemetry, which means every failure mode is fully reproducible and debuggable — not a one-off crash you can't reconstruct.

Full trajectory-planning architecture and vision-model training pipeline are reserved for OEM, research, and flight-test partners under NDA — this page covers what the system does, not how it's built.
