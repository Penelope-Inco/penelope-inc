WHO WE ARE

Strictly aerospace.
Specialists in digital twins.

We operate strictly within the aerospace sector, architecting high-fidelity, physics-informed digital twin technology for vehicle component health monitoring.

Where We Stand

We do not build generic software. Penelope Inc. exists to solve hard problems in aviation telemetry, structural compliance, and predictive maintenance.

Our core focus is embedding physical laws into machine learning — whether that's predicting micro-fractures in a turbine blade or securing the cryptographic integrity of ADS-B transmissions through the PHI-Chain ledger, our architecture is built for zero-leakage, edge-deployable operation.

We also occasionally apply our engineering discipline to community services — but our true north is, and always will be, flight-critical aerospace infrastructure.
