Who We Are

Strictly Aerospace.
Specialists in Digital Twins.

We are Penelope Inc. We operate strictly within the aerospace sector, architecting high-fidelity, physics-informed digital twin technology for vehicle component health monitoring.

Where We Stand

We do not build generic software. Penelope Inc. exists to solve the hardest problems in aviation telemetry, structural compliance, and predictive maintenance.

Our core focus is embedding physical laws into machine learning matrices. Whether it is predicting micro-fractures in an AGTF30 turbine blade or ensuring the cryptographic integrity of ADS-B transmissions via the PHI-Chain ledger, our architecture is designed for zero-leakage, edge-deployable survival.

While we leverage our engineering team to provide community services (like school apps and security audits), our true north is, and always will be, flight-critical aerospace infrastructure.
