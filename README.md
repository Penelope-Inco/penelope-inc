# PHI Lab (Physics Hybrid Integrity Lab)

**DIMASA** — Digital Intelligent Maintenance for Aircraft Systems in Africa.

Aerospace AI Systems Research & Development facility pioneering physics-informed edge digital twins, zero-leakage neural telemetry, and verifiable flight integrity.

### PHI Suites
- **PHI-01 PHI-Twin**: Edge digital twins for landing gear & structural health monitoring.
- **PHI-02 PHI-Chain**: Split-payload blockchain security for aviation communications.
- **PHI-03 PHI-Arc**: Proprietary low-cost, high-standard engineering methodology.
- **PHI-04 PHI-Sense**: 1,027-sensor HUMS fusion array.
- **PHI-05 PHI-Engine**: Rotating detonation & gas turbine research.
- **PHI-06 PHI-Drone**: HIL validation for autonomous UAV trajectory & failure mitigation.
- **PHI-07 PHI-Swarm**: Autonomous collaborative UAV flocking & distributed routing.
- **PHI-08 PHI-Ctrl**: Physics-hybrid fault-tolerant flight control, validated in JSBSim.
- **PHI-09 PHI-Spike**: Neuromorphic event-driven sensing & ultra-low-power SNN processing.
