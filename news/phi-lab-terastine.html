PHI Lab and TerAstine Technology Ltd Join Forces to Advance Aircraft Maintenance AI in Africa

PHI Lab and TerAstine Technology Ltd are collaborating to scale DIMASA, bringing physics-informed digital twin research closer to Africa's maintenance hangars.

PHI Lab (Physics Hybrid Integrity Lab) and TerAstine Technology Ltd today announced a bilateral collaboration to advance aerospace AI research in Africa.

At the centre of the work is DIMASA, Digital Intelligent Maintenance for Aircraft Systems in Africa: PHI Lab's mission to help African maintenance, repair and overhaul (MRO) operations move from scheduled to condition-based maintenance, using physics-informed digital twins that monitor the health of aircraft components.

TerAstine Technology Ltd brings technical capability in edge compute architectures and sovereign systems engineering, complementing PHI Lab's core aerospace testbeds and fault-tolerant flight systems.

Working together under the DIMASA initiative, the two organisations will accelerate condition-based maintenance validation and scale digital twin telemetry from laboratory research directly to hangar deployments.

“African aviation spends millions of dollars on foreign scheduled maintenance cycles. With DIMASA, our goal is to bring physics-informed digital twin prognostics to local hangars, ensuring components are serviced based on actual operational health rather than rigid schedules. Collaborating with TerAstine accelerates that roadmap.”

— Mohammed Bello Sani, Founder of PHI Lab

PHI Lab develops physics-informed digital twins for aircraft component health monitoring, from landing gear prognostics to flight control, with research and testbeds based in Kaduna, Nigeria.

Engineers, researchers, OEMs and MRO operators interested in collaborating can get in touch through our contact portal.
