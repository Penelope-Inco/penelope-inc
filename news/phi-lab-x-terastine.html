PHI Lab x TerAstine Technology Ltd

Strategic aerospace technology collaboration between PHI Lab and TerAstine Technology Ltd focusing on edge digital twins and sovereign avionics systems.

PHI Lab (Physics Hybrid Integrity Lab) and TerAstine Technology Ltd have initiated a formal bilateral engagement to co-develop and evaluate next-generation aerospace maintenance architectures under the DIMASA (Digital Intelligent Maintenance for Aircraft Systems in Africa) framework.

Technical specifications and joint deployment milestones will be announced following validation testing across both facilities.
